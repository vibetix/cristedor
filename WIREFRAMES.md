## 5. Summary of Wireframe Assets & Interactive Components

1. **Header Navigation Bar**: Sticky blur header with dark/light mode toggle and contact button.
2. **Hero Node Canvas**: Interactive background network displaying connected holding nodes.
3. **Division Ecosystem Tabs**: Seamless tab switching between Tech, Media, Edu, Finance, AI, and Future Labs.
4. **Venture Card Grid**: Modular card system with hover elevation and detail drawer triggers.
5. **Investor Filings Table**: Structured downloadable PDF resource table.
6. **Global Footprint Map**: Visual SVG/Canvas world map with interactive HQ hotspots.
7. **Application & Contact Drawers**: Accessible side-slide modals for conversion actions.
